Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
How does the rating system work?
When someone views your profile, they can rate it. You'll see aggregated feedback on your profile page — not who gave which rating. Ratings help surface genuine profiles in discovery.
How do I report someone?
Tap the three-dot menu on any profile or in a chat to report or block. Reports are reviewed by our team. For urgent safety concerns, use the form above and select "Safety concern."
When is Proof available?
Proof launches on iOS on September 1, 2026. Sign up on the home page to be notified the moment it's live.
